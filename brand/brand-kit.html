05 · Voice & graphic language
Principle 01
LIVE SYSTEMS

Not slide decks.

Every claim points at something running. Proof over promise — if it isn't in production, it doesn't go in the copy.

Principle 02
SERIOUS / UNSERIOUS

Piggy name, engineer's hands.

The name is a nursery rhyme; the work is national infrastructure. Playful surface, rigorous underneath — never the reverse.

Principle 03
CARIBBEAN GROUND

Proven in Barbados.

Small island, national scale. Bajan phrasing stays — Yuh Hear Dem, We Outside 246 — grounded, never touristy.
